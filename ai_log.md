Update this file every week. Be honest — this log is graded for the quality of the
reflection, not for how much or how little you used AI.

## The Evidence section is not optional

Every week you must paste the **actual exchange** that backs up your claim about an AI
error: the prompt you sent and the wrong answer you got back. Not the whole
conversation — the ten or fifteen lines that prove it. A claim without evidence does
not earn the marks.

Why: anyone can write "the AI made a mistake and I fixed it". The exchange itself is
the part that cannot be invented convincingly, and noticing which part of a long
conversation is the evidence is itself the skill being assessed.

You may also keep the full transcript in `weekNN/transcript.md` if you like. I will not
read it by default, but I reserve the right to ask for it if something in your log does
not add up.
